Graph-engineered methodology

A number is published only when its path is traceable.

The data system applies the Karpathy-style ratchet described in the graph-engineering paper: inspect the current graph, propose one bounded patch, evaluate it against fixed checks, then keep or revert. Every company, claim, source, calculation and review decision remains addressable across runs.

Split mechanics

The pizza does not get bigger.

01

What a split changes

A 2-for-1 split doubles the share count and roughly halves the price per share. Ownership percentage and company value are unchanged.

02

Why the original value rises

The never-split figure asks what all the descendant shares from one original share are worth at the snapshot price.

03

Why share classes matter

Class restructurings and spin-offs can create more than one security. Those cases require a richer graph than a single multiplication.

04

What reverse splits do

Reverse splits reduce share count and raise price per share. They are represented as fractional factors and reviewed separately.
