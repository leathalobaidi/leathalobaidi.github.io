How to read this edition

This is a dated research catalogue. Financial and appointment observations in the public tables were checked against the cited sources. A catalogue name or a research-profile flag does not establish a verified biography, a current role or every inherited firm attribute.

Research and source review used AI assistance. Original-source links, accounting boundaries and remaining uncertainty are retained so readers can assess the observations. The main research scope contains 106 people; 65 lack an inherited research dossier. Education and specialisms have not been comprehensively collected.

Financial figures mix periods and legal entities. Missing values remain missing, estimates stay labelled, and original currencies are retained. No total across these rows is presented as UK sector revenue.
