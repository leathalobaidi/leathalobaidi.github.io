Structure

Turnover and highest-paid-member values are converted to GBP at €/£ 0.85 and $/£ 0.78 where filed in another currency. The 30 positive turnover rows total £2.4418bn before display rounding and span year-ends from July 2024 to December 2025. This is gross reported turnover for selected legal entities, including non-economics activity inside broad and litigation consultancies; it is not UK sector revenue or a market floor. "Members" and "top earner" come from LLPs' filed accounts, or from Ltd companies that disclose an equivalent partner/member figure; "staff" is average headcount where disclosed. One institute (the Fraser of Allander Institute, structure "Other") sits outside both the LLP and Ltd filter chips and is reachable via "All". The generated explorer has no turnover value for Cebr or the named overseas, parent-filed and sub-brand practices (BDO, Deloitte, PwC, Grant Thornton, RSM economics arms, PA Consulting, E.CA and Fraser of Allander); several also have no headcount value. They remain in the catalogue and table, shown as “—”. “Est.” uses the recorded brand-founding year for 24 catalogue rows (including NERA 1961, CRA 1965 and PA 1943) and the UK incorporation year for the other 66. On that basis, the explorer displays FTI 2012, AlixPartners 2010, A&M 2007, Cornerstone 2014, ICF 2001, EIU 1983, Ecorys 1982 and AFRY 1991. Each firm's CH-derived facts are reconciled in the project ledger.
