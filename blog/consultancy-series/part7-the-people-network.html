The People Network

Part 7 of 7: Selected career moves, with the source behind each appointment.

Leath · Updated 14 November 2026

Stefan Hunt’s two moves connect the CMA, Keystone and AlixPartners. Rameet Sangha’s earlier move connects AlixPartners to Compass Lexecon. Reading the events together makes those paths visible, while the individual sources explain what happened at each step.

This reference contains 11 selected appointments involving 10 people, with recorded dates from January 2020 to January 2026. Ten concern UK or European roles; Faten Sabry’s New York appointment is included as a clearly marked US example. The selection covers events discussed in the series for which a receiving-firm announcement supplies a date and role. It is not a complete record of any firm’s hiring or departures.

Follow the events

Reading the selection

Two colleagues can be named in one release, as Mazzarotto and Wilkinson were; one person can appear in two events, as Hunt does. A later LLP appointment can concern a person who has worked in the same corporate family for years. Keeping those units separate makes the record useful.

Missing names do not establish loyalty or a lack of recruitment. Nor do repeated appearances measure a firm’s importance in the market. The reference lets readers inspect particular routes. Part 6 considers what those routes suggest about demand for regulatory experience; Part 5 examines the financial stakes.
