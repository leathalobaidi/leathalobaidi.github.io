What the dates mean

The observation date records when the address or city was seen. A filing gives the effective date of a registered-office change when the register confirms it. A publication date refers to the notice or statement. The review checked every row on 14 November 2026. The change forms establish effective dates for 66 of the 80 latest register entries.

  • York Aviation changed its registered office on 2 September, Economic Perspectives on 17 September and Calverley Economic Advisors on 23 September. The July addresses remain in the register below. Both 7 September AlixPartners filings are linked to explain the street-number correction.
  • The latest Companies House entries for Vivid Economics and Indepen Consulting give their last registered addresses and their dissolution dates. Indepen’s exact Gazette notice identifies the Sheffield address as the liquidators’ premises.
  • The Cambridge Econometrics contact directory lists Cambridge and Northampton. Brussels and Budapest remain dated September listings, supported by the firm’s July privacy policy. The evidence leaves their current status open.
  • CRA labels its Richardson and Bryan sites Dallas and College Station. Oxera lists a coworking address for Munich. EIU lists four regional contacts on its contact page.

Companies House records the address for legal correspondence. The firm directory rows reproduce a selected set of office and contact listings. Links to page sections use either the original anchor or a text locator that compatible browsers can highlight.
