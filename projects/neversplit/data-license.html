Kaggle cross-check

A modified 59-security subset of Stock Prices Without Splits — Historical Nominal Value, version 15, by Arandkei / Gerardo Luna (2026), is used under CC BY 4.0. NeverSplit extracts cumulative-factor candidates, normalises identifiers and reconciles them with separate issuer and event histories.

Other components

Constituent identity data comes from datasets/s-and-p-500-companies, declared PDDL 1.0. SEC EDGAR sources follow the SEC public-reuse policy. No open redistribution licence was located for the Slickcharts market snapshot or the Ritter academic workbook; those fields are identified in meta.rights.

Company marks

Company marks are served as small local image files rather than commercial runtime hotlinks. One hundred independent audit batches reviewed all 503 marks. Confirmed corrections use documented issuer-hosted assets where accessible, with public identification copies used when issuer delivery blocks automated retrieval. Remaining marks use icon metadata published on issuer websites, Simple Icons under CC0 1.0, or a locally cached resolution from the open Favicon.im API.

The company mark provenance manifest identifies the method, evidence source, asset URL and retrieval date for all 503 securities. The audit found 39 blank, generic, cropped, degraded or obsolete images and replaced each with a documented identification mark. Company names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are shown only to identify the listed company.
