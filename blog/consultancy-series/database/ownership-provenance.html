How to read the dates

Dates are labelled as signing, announcement, completion, filing period, or register notification. Where the source supplies only a month or year, the database preserves that precision. Control conditions are those recorded at Companies House, including any cessation or withdrawal. A control band does not state the precise shareholding, and a notification date does not establish the closing date of a transaction.

The review tracked 189 inherited deal and ownership records and claim lines. The database uses the scope and figures established by the originals. It leaves out unsupported inherited estimates, such as the reported valuation attributed to Flint, and keeps the LECG practice sales separate from any claim that the company filed for bankruptcy.

For example, the Oxford Analytica and Dragonfly purchase agreement was signed on 21 February 2025. The two-business sale closed on 31 March. The UK company’s accounts describe an ultimate-parent change from 1 April. The UK register’s Factiva notification has its own date.
