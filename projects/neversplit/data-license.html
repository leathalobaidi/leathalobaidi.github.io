Kaggle cross-check

A modified 59-security subset of Stock Prices Without Splits — Historical Nominal Value, version 15, by Arandkei / Gerardo Luna (2026), is used under CC BY 4.0. NeverSplit extracts cumulative-factor candidates, normalises identifiers and reconciles them with separate issuer and event histories.

Other components

Constituent identity data comes from datasets/s-and-p-500-companies, declared PDDL 1.0. SEC EDGAR sources follow the SEC public-reuse policy. No open redistribution licence was located for the Slickcharts market snapshot or the Ritter academic workbook; those fields are identified in meta.rights.
