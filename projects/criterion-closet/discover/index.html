Why do 272 Top 10s contain 3,075 titles?
The number 272 counts articles, not films. Ties and grouped selections let a guest fit several titles into one position. These lists are separate from the Closet videos.

*The Closet source rows include one Criterion tote bag. It stays in the archive and is excluded from film rankings. These are structured page selections, not exhaustive transcripts of everything said on video.
