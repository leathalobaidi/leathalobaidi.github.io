How the figures were checked

Codex matched 31 Companies House PDFs to the archived copies byte for byte and read the relevant statements and notes. The review compared the period, value, currency, units and reporting scope with the database. No database values changed. For EKOS, the reported comprehensive income equals profit before tax less the reported tax charge. Comparative rows cite the comparative column in the filing shown. The CSV gives the filing ID, original figure, multiplier where applicable and the PDF’s SHA-256.

The PDF page counts from the start of the file. The printed page is the number on the statement. If the PDF viewer opens at the cover, use the PDF page shown here.

How to read the exceptions

  • Fideres: The LLP reports zero average employees excluding members. The filing provides no average member count, and it explicitly omits the profit and loss account. The amount due to members on the balance sheet is a liability at the reporting date. Both undisclosed observations remain blank in the downloads.
  • Baringa: The estimated highest member entitlement is 7% of the £161,901,443 discretionary profit pool. The estimate excludes a separate entitlement to 8% of qualifying disposal cash. The register links both statement pages.
  • Oxera: Both years use the amended consolidated accounts. The 2024 average member count is 34; the year-end count was 35. Staff counts use the group column.
  • Staff and members: RBB’s group count excludes members. Macro Advisory Partners’ group count includes directors and partners and should not be added to the LLP member count. Profit available for division is distinct from any member’s take-home pay.
