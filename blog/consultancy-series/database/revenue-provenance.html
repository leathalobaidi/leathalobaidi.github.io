How the figures were checked

Codex downloaded the period-specific PDFs from Companies House and confirmed that all 30 matched the archived source files byte for byte. It reread the statement heading, revenue line, currency, units and current-period column from each PDF, then compared the transcribed figure with the existing database row. The CSV records the document SHA-256 for each PDF. The review covers the 30 selected revenue figures. The selection date determines which reporting periods enter the snapshot.

Links request the PDF page number counted from the start of the file. The printed page is the number on the statement. If a viewer opens the cover, go to the cited PDF page.

Accounting choices

  • AlixPartners’ LLP statement shows gross revenue of £281.687m and net revenue of £273.190m after client expenses and disbursements. The selection uses the net line.
  • Oxera’s £70,692,013 comes from the consolidated amended accounts filed on 16 January 2026.
  • Seven filings report the selected line in thousands. The register shows the amount as printed and the multiplier used to express it in whole currency units.
  • A&M Europe filed in euros and Cornerstone Research UK in US dollars. At the project rates of £0.85 per euro and £0.78 per US dollar, the 30 selected observations still total £2,441,511,439.47. The sum combines reporting entities, periods, activities and geographies.
