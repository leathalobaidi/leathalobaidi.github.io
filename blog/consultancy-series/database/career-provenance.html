What the dates mean

We checked the employer announcements, regulator records and Companies House forms against the route, role and date for each event. The event date is an exact day or month according to the source. The CSV separates it from the announcement date and the date of each source where shown.

  • The CMA confirms Mike Walker took up the Frontier role on 5 January 2026. The same day was described as intended in Frontier’s earlier announcement.
  • Compass Lexecon announced Joe Perkins on 14 April 2020, ahead of FTI’s 22 April announcement. Both give an intended June 2020 start. His separate LLP appointment was effective 4 June 2024 and filed 12 July.
  • Keystone’s newsroom dates Stefan Hunt’s first announcement to 16 December 2022; 24 January 2023 is the intended start. AlixPartners’ later announcement and Companies House filing establish two different dates for his second move.
  • The CMA records a September 2025 start for Francesca Sala; 16 September is Oxera’s announcement date, rather than a verified first working day.
