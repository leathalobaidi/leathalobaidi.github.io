How to read the evidence

Companies House supplies dates for statutory appointments and resignations. Employer releases and biographies show the role the issuer actually described. The database preserves their scope, including practice and group names. A profile observation is dated to the review. An announcement date gives the date the news was published, and a start date follows the precision stated by the source.

The 282 inherited affiliation claims keep their original wording in the research database. The table below gives the role and dates supported by the original sources. Inherited roles or dates that the sources did not establish are excluded from the public affiliation table.

Similar names require a link beyond spelling. Mike Walker and the Michael Walker named in CRA’s 2000 announcement share the same professional record. Sharmila Whelan’s professional biography and Sharmila Ghosh’s statutory record remain separate. Vivid’s named liquidators acted in that capacity.
