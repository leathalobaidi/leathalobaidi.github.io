What the dates mean

Incorporation and establishment-opening dates come from Companies House. Earlier trading, practice launches and founding can have different dates. A document date describes the event indicated alongside it. Undated pages carry a review date. The register status is the status observed on the review date.

For matching legal names and standard abbreviations, the register supports the catalogue association. Trading names, practices and group relationships use the original disclosures or filings shown against each link. A legal context link identifies the entity supported by the evidence in its stated scope. An individual engagement may identify a specific contracting party.
